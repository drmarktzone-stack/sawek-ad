### What it can do excellently

- Stay honest: no invented ROAS, likes, ratings, or competitor names.
- Run a **template campaign from intake facts** (name, phone, offer, vertical) with HE+AR+EN ads even when Vertex is down.
- Keep **only three demos**, and keep **Bit/bank secrets off public pages**.
- Speak **HE / AR / EN** as first-class, Desk Noir UI.

### What it cannot do

- Buy ads on Meta / Google / TikTok (plans only).
- Forecast leads or ROAS.
- Ingest a login-walled Facebook/Instagram account as if it were a public page.
- Guarantee Vertex Imagen / Gemini on every request.
- Show the 30-day calendar on Free (Pro gate). The **7-day week is visible** and uses the same hero.
- Be a connected “agency OS”: discovery/strategy/studio/media/leads routes are **department views of the same pack**, not extra live data feeds.

## Root cause: engine desync (confirmed, not assumed)

**Hypothesis held.** Before this PR the “campaign brain” was several independent `intake → generator` pipes:

1. **`assemblePack`** (`lib/engine/run.ts`) built CMO ideas from intake only, a **research skeleton**, and variants from `generateVariants(intake)` — three stories.
2. **`buildAgency`** called `buildCmoIdeasPack(intake)` **again**, so agency calendar/hooks could diverge from `pack.cmoIdeas`.
3. **`overlayPackAgency`** fetched Pro desk + research **in parallel from thin intake facts**. `overlayProOnAgency` could **overwrite calendar themes** with Gemini weeks that ignored vertical (restaurant language on a clinic).
4. **`applyResearchToPack`** only copied grounded notes onto whatever ideas already existed — research never chose or constrained angles.
5. **Viral desk** started with an empty `idea` and rebuilt scripts from that blank, so calendar days 8–30 mixed a *new* viral angle into CMO-stamped days 1–7.
6. **Image offer** queried stock from `category + description` only — not the hero platform.
7. **Result view** stacked Site audit → live previews → all CMO cards → viral desk → channels → variations → angles → agents → score → mission → departments → pillars → diagnosis → research → ads. Ads (the actual deliverable) sat below a salad of tools. Result-view also **rebuilt** CMO ideas if the pack blob was old.

That is why a clinic could pick up olive-table / grill hooks, why calendars felt unrelated to the hero idea, and why the owner saw disconnected panels instead of one agency pack.

---

## Fix shipped in this PR

1. **`CampaignBrief`** (`lib/types.ts`, `lib/engine/campaign-brief.ts`): facts, vertical, voice, geo, offer, gaps, hero idea id, angle ids, core message, viral idea, image queries.
2. **`lib/engine/campaign-orchestrator.ts`**: after ingest, one brief → CMO pack → aligned ad variants (generic/incomplete walls filled from name+phone; vertical leaks stripped) → viral seed → agency.
3. **Research once**, then `attachResearchAndSync` — same hero id, notes attached, no re-pick.
4. **Pro overlay** refuses calendar text that `contradictsVertical`.
5. **Result UI:** hero idea → ads → live previews → **7-day posting week** (always) → research citations. **30-day calendar stays Pro.** Secondary tools (viral, extra CMO platforms, resize, variations, studio, optimizer, departments) sit in one collapsed accordion.
6. QA: `scripts/check-orchestrator.ts` (fictional pediatric clinic + café: one hero across idea / viral / calendar day 1; no restaurant leak on clinic; no incomplete wall when name+phone exist).
